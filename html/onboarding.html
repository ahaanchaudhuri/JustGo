Now try it

Click your address bar and use your shortcut either way:

The fast way
yt/ then Enter

The trailing / matters the first time — it tells Chrome this is an address, not a search. After that, Chrome remembers.

The guided way
go then Tab then yt

Type go and press Tab to search all your shortcuts with suggestions as you type. Type add <name> instead to save the page you're on right now — no panel needed.
